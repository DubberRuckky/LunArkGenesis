# LunArk: Genesis

> A retro pixel-art lunar outpost management simulator for ages 13–16, built for the **NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026**.

In *LunArk: Genesis*, you step into the boots of a lunar outpost commander establishing humanity's permanent foothold on the Moon. Experience real astronautical engineering trade-offs: surviving the 14-Earth-day lunar night, shielding crews from solar cosmic radiation using regolith depth, balancing life support against research milestones, and managing crew boredom before morale collapses.

## 30-Second Quick Start

### Requirements
- **Python**: **3.10** or newer (tested with Python 3.14.6)
- **Engine**: `pygame-ce==2.5.8` (community edition of Pygame)

### Installation & Launch

1. **Clone or navigate into the repository**:
   ```bash
   cd LunArkGenesis
   ```

2. *(Optional but recommended)* **Create and activate a virtual environment**:
   - **Linux / macOS**:
     ```bash
     python3 -m venv .venv
     source .venv/bin/activate
     ```
   - **Windows (PowerShell)**:
     ```powershell
     python -m venv .venv
     .\.venv\Scripts\Activate.ps1
     ```

3. **Install dependencies**:
   ```bash
   pip install -r requirements.txt
   ```

4. **Run the game**:
   - **Linux / macOS**:
     ```bash
     python3 main.py
     ```
   - **Windows**:
     ```powershell
     python main.py
     # or:
     py main.py
     ```

> [!TIP]
> **Stuck in Fullscreen?**
> Press **`F11`** at any time from any screen to instantly toggle between Fullscreen and Windowed mode. Press **`Esc`** in menus to go back, or click the **Quit** button on the Title Screen.

## Settings Menu Explained

Accessible from both the Title Screen and Pause Menu:

- **Display Mode**: Choose between *Fullscreen* (runs at native desktop resolution) and *Windowed*.
- **Resolution**: Available in Windowed mode only (1280×720, 1366×768, 1600×900, 1920×1080, 2560×1440, plus native monitor size).
- **FPS Cap**: Limit refresh rate to 30, 60, 120, 144 FPS, or Unlimited (0). Default is 60 FPS.
- **VSync**: Synchronizes frame output with your display to prevent tearing.
- **Show FPS Counter**: Toggles a lightweight green real-time frame rate readout in the top-right corner.
- **Integer Scaling**: [Default: On] Scales the 480×270 canvas strictly by whole integers (e.g. 2x, 3x, 4x) with crisp pixel preservation, adding clean letterbox bars when needed.
- **10-Second Safety Revert**: Changing your display mode or resolution prompts a 10-second countdown confirmation modal. If the display is black or incompatible, it automatically reverts to your working settings without leaving you stranded.

## How to Play (Student Guide)

As Outpost Commander, you oversee five critical meters:

### The Five Meters
1. **Energy**: Stored battery power. Produced by Fuel Cell Generators, Solar Arrays, or Nuclear Plants. If this reaches zero, base modules shut down in sequence (with life support habitat kept alive longest).
2. **Science**: Research points generated by Laboratories and Planetary Observatories. Required to unlock advanced equipment tiers and fulfill victory conditions.
3. **Boredom**: Crew psychological stress (0–100). Creeps upward over time; reduced by recreational modules like Gyms and Cafes.
4. **Revenue**: Financial budget. Earned through Helium-3 extraction and research grants; spent on excavation and base expansion.
5. **Event Bar**: Operational hazard probability (0–100). Rises when Boredom is high or base systems malfunction, escalating the risk of solar storms and equipment breakdowns.

### Subsurface Digging & Radiation Shielding
The Moon has no magnetic field or substantial atmosphere. Surface modules take the brunt of cosmic radiation (1.0 exposure). By excavating underground into the lunar regolith, radiation exposure drops significantly (down to 0.05), providing natural planetary shielding for your crew!

### Lunar Day & Night Cycle
The Moon experiences ~14 Earth days of continuous sunlight followed by ~14 Earth days of total darkness. Solar Arrays generate zero power during the lunar night! Prepare battery reserves and baseline generators before dusk arrives.

## The Real Science Behind LunArk

- **Fuel Cells over Combustion**: There is no atmospheric oxygen on the Moon. Traditional fossil-fuel or diesel combustion is impossible. LunArk uses Hydrogen-Oxygen fuel cells where reactants produce energy and pure water byproduct.
- **Helium-3 ($^3\text{He}$)**: Solar wind has deposited Helium-3 into the lunar regolith for billions of years. Helium-3 is a prospective clean fuel for future aneutronic nuclear fusion power on Earth and in deep space.
- **In-Situ Resource Utilization (ISRU) Shielding**: Lunar soil (regolith) is rich in silicates, iron, and titanium oxides. Placing crew modules under 2–3 meters of regolith absorbs harmful ionizing cosmic rays and solar particle events.

## "How To" Guides

### How to Run Tests
Execute the unit test suite via:
```bash
python -m pytest tests/ -v
```

### How to Add Custom Art
1. Check `LUNARK_ASSET_GUIDE.md` for dimensions and coordinates.
2. Save your PNG file in the matching path in `img/`.
3. Launch the game — it will immediately use your real art instead of the procedural placeholder!

### How to Add a Custom Font
Drop your TrueType font into `fonts/pixel.ttf`. The game will automatically detect and load it on next launch.

### How to Add a New Screen
1. Create `game/ui/screens/myscreen.py` with `handle_events`, `update(dt)`, and `draw(surface)`.
2. Register the screen class in `game/app.py` inside `self.screen_registry`.
3. Transition to it from any screen with `self.app.set_screen("myscreen")` or `self.app.push_screen("myscreen")`.
